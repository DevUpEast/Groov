Groov / Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

Groov is a habit-tracking app for iOS and Android. This policy explains what information Groov collects, why, where it goes, and the choices you have. We keep it short because the list is short: Groov collects only what it needs to keep your habits in sync across your devices and to coach you.

What we collect

Account information

  • Email address and password when you create an account with email. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider and are never visible to us.
  • Name and email from Apple or Google when you sign in with those services. Apple lets you hide your real email; we only receive what you approve.
  • Display name, which you can edit in Settings. It is used to greet you and to personalise coaching messages.

Your habit data

  • Habits you create (name, icon, type, goal, reminder time).
  • Daily completions and the list of habits that existed each day.
  • Challenges, their progress and the ones you have finished.
  • App settings (theme, notification, sound and haptic preferences, reminder times).

Photos

If you choose a photo as a habit icon, Groov asks for photo library or camera access at that moment. The image stays on your device. Groov stores only the file path on our servers so the habit can be re-matched to the image on the same device.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, a report is sent to Sentry, our crash-reporting provider. A report contains the error and stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version and a random installation identifier. It does not contain your habits, completions or messages.

What we do not collect

Groov has no advertising, no advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics, and does not collect your location, contacts or precise device identifiers. Reminders are scheduled on your device; we do not hold push-notification tokens.

How we use it

  • Sync. Your habit data is stored with Supabase so it is available on every device you sign in from and survives a reinstall.
  • AI coaching. To write the daily nudge and the weekly or monthly reflection, our server sends your habit names, completion statistics for the recent period, how long you have used Groov, and your display name to Anthropic's Claude API. Anthropic processes this under its commercial API terms, which do not permit using the data to train its models. The generated message is cached on our server so it is not regenerated every time you open the app.
  • Support and safety. Crash reports help us find and fix bugs.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with anyone other than the service providers listed here, who process it only on our instructions.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept for as long as you have an account. Clear All Data in Settings deletes your habit data but keeps the account. Delete Account in Settings permanently removes your account, all habit data and cached coaching messages from our servers immediately. Crash reports are retained by Sentry for 90 days.

Your choices

  • Delete your habit data or your whole account from Settings at any time, without contacting us.
  • Turn off notifications, sounds or haptics in Settings or in your device settings.
  • Revoke photo access in your device settings; existing habits keep working with their emoji icon.
  • If you are in the EU, UK, California or another region with privacy rights, you can also ask us to access, correct or delete your data, or object to processing, by contacting us below.

Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Database rows are protected by row-level security so an account can only read and write its own records. The AI key used for coaching lives only on our server and is never shipped inside the app.

Children

Groov is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will update the date above and mention it in the app's release notes. The current version always lives at this address.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/DevUpEast/Groov/issues or use the support contact on the app's store listing.
